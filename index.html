Naalaya Theerpu Song

Naalaiya Theerpu (transl.  Tomorrow's Verdict) is a 1992 Indian Tamil-language vigilante action film directed by S. A. Chandrasekhar and produced by his wife Shoba Chandrasekaran, which marked the debut of their son Vijay as a leading actor. The film also stars Keerthana, Easwari Rao, Srividya, Radha Ravi and Sarath Babu. The music was composed by newcomer Manimekalai with cinematography by R. P. Imayavaramban and editing by Gautham Raju. The film revolves around a businessman who has many extramarital affairs and molests his wife. However, things take an unexpected turn when she becomes pregnant and has a son who later avenges his father's deeds. The film was released on 4 December 1992 to mostly negative reviews and became a box-office bomb. However, Vijay won the Cinema Express Award for Best New Face Actor.

The first major career-changing and breakthrough movie for Thalapathy Vijay is the 1996 romantic comedy-drama "Poove Unakkaga."

Major Career Turning Points While Vijay started as a child artist and had early leads directed by his father, a few milestone movies completely reshaped his trajectory:Poove Unakkaga (1996): Directed by Vikraman, this film became Vijay's first major commercial blockbuster. It transformed him into a bankable romantic hero and won over family audiences.Ghilli (2004): Directed by Dharani, this action-masala film took his mass-action appeal and superstardom to an entirely different level, cementing his status as a top-tier box office king.Thuppakki (2012): Directed by A.R. Murugadoss, this stylish action thriller modernized his image for contemporary audiences and kicked off a string of massive modern box office hits.
